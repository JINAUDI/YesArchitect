Sketch Process

Every great space begins with a single line.

Before form becomes fixed, we test proportion, rhythm and intent through fast architectural studies.

A loose study keeps the idea alive while scale, circulation and silhouette are still open to move.

01 / Concept Study

Initial hand sketches let us compare massing, movement, light and visual balance before committing to detailed drawings.

massing proportion movement light
02 / Development

The sketch becomes a precise architectural language.

  • How long does an interior design project usually take?
    Project duration depends on scope. We begin with requirements, site context and circulation, then move through concept design, drawings and documentation.
  • Can you work within my budget?
    Yes. We align planning, material selection and drawing detail with the client budget and the level of finish required for the project.
  • Do you provide 3D design or visualization before starting the work?
    Yes. Conceptual planning and visual direction can be prepared before detailed drawings, especially for hospitality and interior projects.
  • Do you handle both residential and commercial projects?
    Yes. The studio works across hospitality, residential, office and proposal projects, with a strong specialization in hospitality spaces.
